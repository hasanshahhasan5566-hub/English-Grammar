Parts of Speech:

There are eight(8) parts of Speech.Which are given below;

  1. Noun
  2. Pronoun
  3. Verb
  4. Adjective
  5. Adverb
  6. Preposition
  7. Cunjunction
  8. Interjection
go to Definitions

Definitions Of Parts Of Speech:-


1.🧑NOUN

Noun is the name of a person place or thing.

Examples:

  1. Aamir🥰
  2. Mian Essa🏚
  3. Bat🏏

2.🔄PRONOUN

pronoun indicates to noun.OR To use instead of a noun.

Examples:

  1. I👩‍🎓
  2. YOU🧍‍♀️
  3. THEY👩🏼‍🤝‍👩🏻
  4. WE👩‍👩‍👦‍👦
  5. HE🧒
  6. SHE👩‍🦰
  7. IT😻

3.🏃VERB

verb is an action or a state.

Examples:

  1. Go🏃‍♀️
  2. Play🏏
  3. Eat🧏‍♂️

4.🎨ADJECTIVE

Adjective modifies a Noun or a Pronoun.

Examples:

  1. GOOD👍
  2. BAD👎
  3. NICE👌

5.🚀ADVERB

Adverb modifies a noun,pronoun,adjective or another adverb.

Examples:
  1. LOVELY💖
  2. SWEETLY🍜
  3. SIMPLY👨

6.📍PREPOSITION

Preposition shows the relation of one word with another.

Examples:
  1. WITH🤝
  2. ON✍
  3. IN👼

7.🔗CUNJUNTION

A conjunction joins words or sentences.

  1. and🏋️‍♂️
  2. or💁‍♂️
  3. but👇

8. 😮 Interjection

An interjection expresses sudden feelings.

Examples:

  1. Hurray! 🎉
  2. Oh! 😮
  3. Wow! 🤩
